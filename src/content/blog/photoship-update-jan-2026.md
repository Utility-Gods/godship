## Excel Import

Got products in a spreadsheet? The **Excel Import** feature lets you:

1. Upload your Excel file (.xlsx, .xls)
2. AI auto-detects columns and maps fields
3. Preview matches with existing products
4. Choose to update existing or create new products

Supports **embedded images in cells**, image URLs, and preserves custom fields. This pairs perfectly with our industry templates - download, customize, import.

## AI-Powered PDF Extraction

Already have catalogues in PDF format? Upload them and let AI do the heavy lifting:

- **Product names and descriptions** extracted automatically
- **Prices and SKU codes** identified
- **Specifications and dimensions** parsed
- **Categories and tags** suggested
- **Images extracted** from PDF pages

Upload → Process → Review → Import. What used to take hours of manual data entry now happens in minutes.

## Bulk Image Upload with AI

Drop up to **10 product images** at once and AI analyzes each one:

- Generates **product names** (2-5 words)
- Writes **detailed descriptions**
- Suggests **categories and tags**
- Creates **unique product IDs**

Choose from industry templates to customize what fields AI generates, or let it auto-detect everything. Perfect for quickly digitizing physical inventory.

## Online Store Snapshots

When you generate a PDF or create an online store link, Photoship now captures a **frozen snapshot** of your catalogue data. This means:

- The PDF shows exactly what your catalogue looked like when generated
- "View Online" links in PDFs display the same snapshot
- No surprises if you update products after sharing

This is particularly useful for wholesale catalogues where pricing needs to match what was sent to buyers.
